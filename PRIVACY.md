# Canvas Harness privacy

Applies to Canvas Harness 3.6.0 through 4.0 Preview 2, which the browser shows as version 3.99.2. Last updated October 30, 2026.

Canvas Harness is a browser extension maintained by Flzsh. It displays information from the Canvas account already signed in on your school's website. It has no developer-operated account service, analytics collector or tracking endpoint in the extension code for this release. From 4.0 Preview 2, **Your AI** can send Canvas text to an AI service that you choose; see [Your AI](#your-ai-what-goes-where).

## Canvas access and local storage

The optional Deadline changes feature keeps a bounded, separate observation record per Canvas site and account: assignment/course identifiers and names, due dates, detection times and read flags. It keeps at most 100 changes from the past 30 days and 10,000 baseline assignments, makes no additional Canvas requests and adds no permissions. It is not included in personal workspace backups. Disabling the feature stops observation while retaining its saved record.

- Canvas Harness reads courses, assignments, due dates, teacher materials, announcements, Canvas Inbox messages and grades that your signed-in Canvas account is allowed to view. Canvas API requests are read-only. Inbox messages are shown in the Course hub, and read by Your AI, without changing their read status. Before 4.0 Preview 2 they were never included in AI context; from 4.0 Preview 2, Your AI can include parts of them, as described below.
- Your Canvas profile is read to greet you by name and to keep each account's data apart. From 3.8.0, only your name and Canvas account ID are kept, cached on this device; your email address, login and SIS IDs, profile picture and calendar feed address are not saved. Versions 3.6.0 and 3.7.0 cached the whole profile response on this device; 3.8.0 replaces it at the next refresh.
- The extension can cache fetched Canvas information, including course and assignment details and grade data, in your browser. Personal notes, plans, bookmarks, homework check-offs, imported course artwork, layout choices, GPA preferences and the study companion's name, look and progress counters are also stored locally.
- Your look settings (light or dark theme, motion, artwork at night, whether the pony is shown) are kept once for this browser, not per account. So the opening screen can be dark from its first moment, the word "dark" (and nothing else) is remembered in the Canvas site's own browser storage while dark is chosen; choosing light removes it.
- The extension uses Chrome/Edge extension local storage and separates saved data by school Canvas origin and account ID. It does not send these records to a Canvas Harness developer server or synchronize them through a Canvas Harness account.
- Cached coursework becomes visible only after the signed-in Canvas account is verified. Returning to an idle tab temporarily hides private content while the account is checked again; a different account cannot inherit the previous account's visible workspace. The one exception is a switch you control: **Open at once** (off unless you turn it on) shows your saved workspace immediately on load and on returning to the tab while the same account check runs, and closes the workspace if the check fails. While it is on, the extension also remembers the account ID that last opened the workspace on that Canvas site. Leave it off on a shared computer.
- Grades are hidden by default in the interface, but hiding them does not necessarily remove them from the local cache. GPA and what-if calculations run locally and are never sent to an AI. Scores and grades reach an AI only when you turn the matching switches on, as described under Your AI.
- Checking off homework or making a personal plan changes only your local Canvas Harness data. It does not submit homework, edit a Canvas assignment or change your Canvas grade.

Personal backups are files that you choose to export. They can contain private notes, preferences, artwork and other personal information. From 4.0 Preview 2 they also contain Your AI's settings, its memory and its record of the last 14 days of runs, including the Canvas text each run used and what it changed. They do not contain API keys, API settings, Help cards, study aids or Overnight folder access. Keep them private. The public extension package and example previews should contain only shared code, generic artwork and artificial example data, never your exported backup or personal course records.

## Feature choices

Full, Lite and Custom choices are stored once for this extension in this browser, separately from your Canvas account data. Dependencies are included automatically. Turning a feature off changes which packaged modules load on your next Canvas page load; it does not erase notes, artwork, plans, GPA settings or reader annotations. Existing pages keep their loaded selection until reload.

The Canvas site's local storage holds a small feature-selection hint for early drawings. It contains preset and feature IDs, with no account or coursework data. The worker keeps the current document's selection in extension session storage across worker restarts and removes its records when the tab closes. An origin that was closed when preferences changed can briefly use its previous hint until the extension confirms the new selection.

## Pictures and videos

Your own pictures and short videos used as banners, card art or a dimmed page background stay in your browser, separated by Canvas site and account. Upload, drag and drop, and paste read only the files or clipboard items you choose. Pictures are scaled to WebP and large videos are shrunk on your device; SVGs pass the existing safety checks. Nothing is uploaded to Canvas, the developer, the Market or an AI provider. Framing, loops, stills and media bytes can be included in kits and personal backups that you choose to export. Lite keeps saved media and backups without loading media decoding or playback modules.

## Market and files you import

The Market is an optional static website at flzsh.github.io. Opening it from the popup asks the browser for access to that site; it is not a required permission. The extension registers a small packaged bridge only on the Market's own path and removes it when permission is withdrawn. The Market can report which of its public pack IDs are installed, so it can show an installed state. It does not receive your Canvas origin, account ID, courses, assignments, grades, notes, routines, backups or other personal records.

Choosing an install downloads that pack's data and inline assets. Canvas Harness checks the declared file size and SHA-256 hash before parsing it, and checks embedded asset hashes and formats too. Supported packs contain course artwork, theme or pet drawings, local layouts, Planner templates, routines and setting choices. A feature pack can only switch on code already included in the extension. Downloaded JavaScript is never run; unknown pack kinds are skipped. SVGs are checked and shown in isolated images. Pack assets have no external URLs to fetch.

Imported packs, receipts and media stay in extension storage and IndexedDB, separated by the student's own Canvas site and account where applicable. Installing or removing account data verifies that school's current signed-in account with a read-only Canvas profile request. Nothing personal is sent to the Market or the developer. As with visiting any website, the Market host receives the normal request for the page and selected download. No telemetry or coursework upload is added.

Add from a file reads the file you choose locally. Personal backups include checked theme, pet and preset documents, saved kits and their media bytes, and account-specific Market receipts. Restore checks formats and hashes before saving. The browser's Full/Lite/Custom choice stays on that device. Remove and Undo use the saved install receipt to restore the settings and artwork that the pack replaced. Exported backups can contain these assets as well as your personal data; keep them private.

Biscuit's language parser, focus timers, reminders, skills, routines and quiet time run locally. A line Biscuit cannot answer can be handed to Your AI when that feature is on: it opens **Tools → AI** with your question filled in and does not press Send. Scene themes and play tools use packaged assets. Reduced motion and hidden-page handling pause animated scenes.

## My links & files

Personal course links and files stay in this browser, separated by Canvas site, account and course. Nothing is uploaded to Canvas, the developer or any other service. Files are kept locally in IndexedDB, up to 25 MB each and 250 MB per account. Opening a saved link visits its destination. PDFs open locally in the workspace reader; images and text open in a local viewer, and other documents download with their original filename. Local PDFs are not offered to the AI handoff.

Backups include links, filenames and file bytes within the 66.5 MB backup cap, and say when files are left out because they are unavailable or do not fit. Keep exported backups private. Canvas-site file storage can be removed by clearing that site's browser data.

## PDF reader

Clicking a PDF link or preview on Instructure Canvas opens the workspace reader, including Files rows, file addresses, modules, the Course hub and My links & files. Unlabelled Canvas file links use one read-only file-metadata request to identify PDFs. Quiz pages do not activate the reader. It requests that chosen file through the signed-in Canvas session and reads its bytes, document name and page text locally for display, selection and search. The reader accepts files up to 32 MiB. Saved local PDFs on trusted custom Canvas domains use the same reader through randomized extension URLs; ordinary Canvas links there retain the original viewer. A blocked or unsupported download falls back to the original viewer or link. The reader does not upload the original PDF to an AI provider.

Highlights, underlines, strike-through, sticky notes, text boxes, shapes, pen and highlighter strokes are saved in extension local storage on this device, separated by Canvas origin, verified account ID and file. Marks load only after sign-in verification and are hidden while the account is rechecked after returning to a hidden tab. Older site-and-file marks move once to the first verified account that opens that file, and the old record is deleted. These annotations do not change the original Canvas file. A copy you explicitly save with marks can contain them. The reader also remembers its split width, the last page per verified account and file, and each tool's colour, width or text size on this device. It uses the browser's saved appearance choice. Copy as notes sends your marks to the clipboard only when pressed; Download .md saves them locally as a Markdown study sheet after rechecking the account. Text marks are grouped by page and position; document text stays literal, safe filenames are used, and drawings or empty marks are counted as omitted. These exports stay on the device unless you choose to share them. Open in Canvas's own viewer remembers that choice for the tab's session, with a button to switch back.

Reading or annotating a PDF does not share its text with an AI provider. Pressing **Ask AI** prepares only your chosen text or page text, with the document name and page reference, in the local AI pane after verifying your Canvas sign-in. Your annotations are not included. That chosen text leaves for the selected AI provider only when you then press **Send**. Review it first: the PDF's own words or filename may contain personal information.

HTTPS links to PDFs on other websites can open in the reader. When access is needed, the existing optional `https://*/*` permission is requested once for that exact site; refusing opens the original viewer or link and prevents repeated prompts. No install-time permission is added. External PDF requests omit credentials and do not send Canvas account information. The external host receives the selected file request. Annotations and reading position remain separated by the verified Canvas account and file.

## Your AI: what goes where

Your AI is part of the Full preset, which is the default, and is left out of Lite. Its main switch, **Your AI** in **Tools → AI → ···**, starts **on**; **Prepare every day**, beside it, starts **off**. It reaches an AI in one of three ways, chosen under **Way**: the AI's own website, your own API key, or a folder on your computer that an AI tool of your own reads. Canvas Harness has no AI service of its own. Step-by-step use is in [Study with AI](AI.md).

### When text leaves this device

- **On its own, only if you chose it.** The first time the Help band shows on Home, it asks once: **Every day** or **Only when I ask**. Until you choose, and after **Only when I ask**, nothing is sent on its own; this also holds for anyone who used an earlier preview. With **Every day** (the **Prepare every day** switch in Tools → AI → ···), when you open Canvas, Prepare reads your Canvas on this device on the first visit of the day, and again when your assignments, files, announcements, Inbox or calendar change, up to **Daily cap** runs a day (3 unless you change it; 0 stops these runs). It then sends its message through your chosen way without another click, but only if that way is ready: you have already allowed Canvas Harness on that AI website, saved an API key and allowed its address, or chosen and allowed an Overnight folder. Until then nothing leaves the device and the Help band says what is missing. Allowing a website once, for example to send one question, does not turn this on. To stop it, choose **Only when I ask** (turn **Prepare every day** off), turn **Your AI** off, or set **Daily cap** to 0.
- **When you click** **Daily AI help**, **Help with this**, **Help with…**, **Ask**, **More** or **Check my work** on a Help card, or **Ask your AI** in Tools → AI. These send without a separate review step.
- **When you press Send in Tools → AI** for a study aid, a PDF's pages or a question from Biscuit. **Review text to share** shows the text first. With an AI website, the text is put in its chat box, and **the website receives it there even before you submit the chat**; you submit it unless **Send automatically** (off by default) is on. With an API key, Send sends it.

Your AI does not run on a quiz-taking page. Changing a Your AI setting, **Show grades** or **Teacher feedback** stops a run in progress.

### What is sent

- **Prepare and Help cards** send a Canvas brief built on this device: your course names; overdue work not handed in and work due in the next three weeks, with due dates, points possible, how it is handed in and Canvas's missing or late mark; instructions for work due in the next seven days; announcements from the last ten days; course calendar events for the next 14 days; for each Canvas Inbox conversation with activity in the last seven days whose newest message is by a teacher or TA of that course (as Canvas lists the course's staff), its subject and up to 300 characters of that message, with classmates in it named only as `[classmate]` (a conversation whose newest message is by a classmate, by you, or by someone Canvas Harness cannot show to be course staff is left out); the names of recent course files and pages; and, only with **Teacher feedback** on (off by default), the latest comment by a teacher or TA on your submission for recent assignments (never your own comments). Lines that look like login details, email addresses and web-address query strings are removed, and the brief has no scores. For each item it helps with, it adds the teacher's instructions, files and pages attached to the item, the course home page or syllabus, course files the AI asks for, text you pasted, your instructions for Prepare and the Remember notes that apply to it. A question adds your own words and the card you asked about.
- **Ask your AI** and Overnight's agent file send a snapshot of your coming days: your courses and upcoming assignments with their instructions and rubrics; with **Teacher feedback** on, comments by a teacher or TA on your submissions (never your own); Inbox messages from the last 14 days by a teacher or TA of the course (a classmate's message is never sent); course calendar events; and your own Canvas Harness data: planner blocks, finished work, bookmarks, notes (up to 6,000 characters), a focus-timer summary, your Home layout, your AI's memory, your recent study aids with practice questions you missed, and your answers to its questions.
- **Send in Tools → AI** sends only the material you selected and reviewed, and your question.
- In the materials read for an item, in the snapshot and in the brief's Inbox lines, classmates' names that appear in Canvas data are replaced with `[classmate]`. Score-like text is removed, and course grades are left out, unless both **Show grades** and **Let the AI see my grades** are on; both start off. With Send in Tools → AI, **Show grades** alone decides it.
- PDFs are read on this device and only their text is sent. Each PDF read for the AI is limited to 2 MiB, 20 pages and 60,000 characters, with no OCR for scanned pages. Text you choose in Tools → AI is limited to 500,000 characters by default, and your question to 20,000.
- **Never sent:** your Canvas password or session cookies, GPA and what-if calculations, PDF annotations, files in My links & files, artwork, personal backups, the files or text you submitted, and classmates' discussion posts (discussions add only the teacher's posts and your own).

### Where it goes

- **An AI website** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen or Z.ai): the message goes into a chat in your own account there, under that provider's terms. For Prepare and Help cards, Canvas Harness opens the site in a background tab beside Canvas, starts a new chat, puts the message in the chat box and presses the site's Send button. Those chats are opened as temporary chats on ChatGPT and incognito chats on Claude, which those sites keep out of your chat history; on the other sites they are normal chats. Ask your AI puts its message, and presses Send, in the website tab Canvas Harness opened from that Canvas tab (or opens one), brings that tab to the front, and uses a normal chat. Canvas Harness reads the reply from the page. For Prepare and Help cards on ChatGPT and Claude, it can also read the reply from the site's record of that chat with your signed-in session; on ChatGPT, when the tab has not shown the chat's address yet, it lists your six newest chats (their names and times, not their messages) and reads only the one created when its message was sent; on Claude it looks up your account's organizations to reach the chat it started. It keeps only the reply after its own message and does not open your other chats. The session token is used for these reads and not saved. Canvas Harness does not read your passwords or cookies.
- **An API provider**: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or the HTTPS address you entered for an OpenAI-compatible service. The extension's background worker sends the message straight to that address with your key in the request header and no cookies. The provider bills you for each request. **Estimate** with Anthropic sends the message to Anthropic's token-counting service; other providers' estimates are made on this device. OpenAI requests ask OpenAI not to store the response. **Daily limit** (20 a day unless you change it) caps answer requests per Canvas account.
- **Your Overnight folder**: a folder you picked with the browser's folder picker and allowed for reading and writing. **Prepare now** writes `prep-YYYY-MM-DD.json`, with the study message for each assignment due in the next two days. With this way, **Ask your AI** writes `agent-YYYY-MM-DD.json`, with its snapshot; a Canvas visit writes one too if you chose **Every day**. Canvas Harness reads the answer files your own AI tool writes there (`…answers.json`, `…actions.json`). It never uploads or deletes these files. What your tool does with them is up to that tool and its provider.
- **Google Docs**, on GitHub (unpacked) installs only: if you allow `docs.google.com`, Canvas Harness reads the text of up to three Google Docs an assignment links, using your Google sign-in, and adds it to that item's material. Chrome Web Store installs never ask for this; you can paste the text on the card instead.

### API keys

An API key is kept in the extension's own database on this device, separately for each Canvas site and account, provider and address. Canvas pages never receive it. Only the extension's background worker reads it, to put it in requests to that provider. The key's last four characters, the provider, address, model, daily limit and the day's request count are kept in extension storage. Keys and these API settings are not in personal backups and are never sent to the developer. **Remove key** deletes the key for the chosen provider.

### What your AI can change

Your AI changes only Canvas Harness's own data on this device: the Today list, planner blocks, bookmarks, a note added to your notes, reminders, which Home modules show, up to three AI modules on Home, highlights in a course PDF, study aids, review dates, its memory (up to 30 facts) and one question for you per run. It cannot write to Canvas, submit work, post, send messages or change grades. Each change is listed under **What your AI did** in Tools → AI with **Undo**, and each memory with **Delete**.

### Saved on this device, and for how long

- **Help cards and study aids**, per Canvas site and account: each day's Help cards are kept, back to 30 days before your newest card; other saved aids keep the newest 40; if the record grows past about 900 KB the oldest go first. Your instructions and up to 40 Remember notes are kept with them. Not in personal backups.
- **Run records** in the extension's database: a run's messages and replies are deleted about ten minutes after it finishes, its Canvas brief is kept so a question later that day can reuse it, and finished runs are deleted after 14 days. A run waiting for you keeps its messages until it finishes, is replaced or is stopped.
- **Your AI's history** in your account's Canvas Harness data: settings, the last 14 days of runs with the Canvas snapshot text each used and what it changed, Today, memory and questions. Included in personal backups.
- **Overnight**: the folder access, the path you typed (used only to fill in the instruction you can copy) and a record of the files written and read. Not in personal backups.
- **Session storage**: which browser tab holds the AI chat, with no message text. Cleared when the browser closes.
- The question you are typing and PDF text you attach in Tools → AI stay in the open panel's memory and are not saved as a conversation or put in a personal backup. Provider chat history follows that provider's own rules.

## Browser permissions

Canvas Harness uses `storage` and `unlimitedStorage` for local data (so caches, artwork and notes are not cut off by the browser's default 10 MB extension limit), `offscreen` for local PDF text extraction and bounded pack-media validation, required `scripting` for the feature loader, `alarms` for Your AI (below), and access to HTTPS `*.instructure.com` Canvas pages. The loader injects only extension-packaged files selected by Full, Lite or Custom, in the extension's isolated world; it does not download executable code. It does not request Canvas passwords or account API keys.

Some schools run Canvas on their own address, such as `canvas.your-school.edu`. If you enter one in the popup, the browser asks you to allow Canvas Harness on **that exact site**, using the already-required `scripting` permission to run there. Only then does it run on that site, and only on pages that are Canvas. The manifest lists `https://*/*` as an optional host permission so that any school's address can be asked for; Canvas Harness requests one origin at a time and never asks for access to all websites. Removing the site in the popup or in the browser's extension site settings stops it running there.

Using an AI website asks for optional access to **that provider's exact origin**; `scripting` is already required by the feature loader. The allowed origins are `chatgpt.com`, `claude.ai`, `gemini.google.com`, `grok.com`, `chat.deepseek.com`, `www.kimi.com`, `chat.qwen.ai` and `chat.z.ai`. With that access, Canvas Harness puts text in the site's chat box, presses its Send button for Your AI requests and reads replies, as described above. The access lets the daily Prepare run send on its own only if you chose **Every day**; otherwise the site is used only for requests you start yourself. Using **Copy** and pasting yourself needs no access.

The API way asks for optional access to one API address when you save it: `api.anthropic.com`, `api.openai.com` or `generativelanguage.googleapis.com`, or, for an OpenAI-compatible service, the one HTTPS address you entered, requested through the optional `https://*/*` permission. GitHub installs can also ask for `docs.google.com` through the same permission, only after you press **Read** on a Help card and then **Allow**. The optional `https://flzsh.github.io/*` permission is for the Market (above).

`alarms` keeps one alarm, set a minute ahead, only while a Your AI run is unfinished, so the run can continue if the browser pauses the extension's background worker. It is cleared when no run is unfinished. It never starts a new run and is not used for Overnight. An unfinished run also continues when the browser starts again, and waits for an open Canvas page before reading Canvas.

You can remove website, API and Google Docs access in your browser's extension site settings. Without it, nothing is sent there.

Canvas Harness does not request access to all websites, cookie-reading, debugger or native-messaging permissions. Your AI does not use a separate login, OAuth connection or companion service; an API key is used only if you add one.

## Other websites and files

Opening **Tools → Calculator** loads Desmos's external graphing or scientific calculator in a frame. Canvas Harness does not pass your Canvas account or coursework to Desmos; Desmos can process its own site traffic under its privacy policy. Opening saved links also visits the destination website.

Some Canvas file downloads redirect from your school's Canvas site to an external file host chosen by Canvas. The extension requests the selected file from Canvas using your signed-in session, limits download size and does not add Canvas credentials to an external host request. If that browser download cannot be read, you can use Canvas to download the file yourself.

Imported SVG artwork is checked for unsafe content and saved in your browser. It is not sent to the developer or included in public release files. Keep privately customized art and backups out of public uploads.

Quick Upload reads pasted artwork packs or files you choose/drop locally. It previews validated images and course destinations before you apply them. Reading and reviewing a pack does not store it or transmit it to a service. Apply changes only the selected artwork and explicit banner fit/position options; one Undo is available while the page remains open, provided the affected artwork has not since changed.

## Removing data and contacting the developer

You can choose **Only when I ask** (or turn **Prepare every day** off) or turn **Your AI** off in Tools → AI, turn off **Your AI & study aids** in the popup's Features (or choose Lite), use **Remove key** for an API key, and revoke optional AI website and API permissions in your browser's extension settings. Removing the extension generally removes its extension-managed local storage, including its databases and any API keys; separately exported backups, files you saved and files in your Overnight folder remain wherever you put them. Messages sent to an AI website or API provider must be managed with that provider.

Developer: **Flzsh**. For questions or privacy reports, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Flzsh/canvas-harness/issues). Do not post personal Canvas information in a public issue.
