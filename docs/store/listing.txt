Canvas Harness

Short description
Bring Canvas courses, assignments, teacher updates, bookmarks and study tools into a workspace you can arrange yourself.

Description
Canvas Harness turns your Canvas dashboard into a workspace you arrange yourself. Your courses, assignments, teacher updates and study tools sit on one page, and the original Canvas pages stay one click away.

WHAT YOU GET

• A Home you build. Add Today, This week, a Focus timer, Clock, Notes, Bookmarks, Grades and Messages beside your coursework. Resize them and drag them where you want.

• Assignments. The assignment list beside its instructions, with due dates, teacher updates and bookmarks close by. Mark homework done locally, including work you hand in outside Canvas.

• Planner. A week of cards. Type "essay outline fri 45m #english" and it lands on Friday, 45 minutes, in English. Drag work to the day you will do it and tick it off. Canvas due dates never change.

• Study companion. A small pony that tells you what is next, studies beside your timer and cheers finished work. It never scolds, and you can switch it off.

• Your own look. Hand-drawn course artwork, outlines, colors, text size and layout presets. Upload your own SVG banners, icons, borders and titles with Quick Upload.

• Study tools. A Desmos calculator, GPA estimates, and an optional AI hand-off where you choose exactly what is shared.

HOW IT WORKS

Open your school's Canvas dashboard and the workspace appears. The "Canvas dashboard" button returns to the original page at any time. To move something, hold its heading until the circle closes, then drag. Undo puts it back. Keyboard controls and reduced-motion preferences are supported.

PRIVACY

• Reads Canvas through the session you are already signed in to. It never changes submissions or grades.
• Plans, notes, bookmarks, artwork and settings stay in your browser. No ads, no analytics, no developer server.
• By default nothing saved is shown until Canvas confirms who is signed in.
• The optional AI tool sends only the material you select to the AI website you choose, after you review it. Desmos loads from its own website when opened.

GOOD TO KNOW

• Works on school Canvas sites under *.instructure.com. Custom Canvas domains are not supported yet.
• What you can see depends on your school's Canvas permissions.
• GPA values are estimates, not official transcript calculations.
• The AI tool may need an account with the provider, and changes to their websites can affect it.
