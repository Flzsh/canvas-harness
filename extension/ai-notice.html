Before you turn on AI help

AI help reads the Canvas coursework you choose (assignments, instructions, course files and announcements) and sends it to the AI chat site you pick, so it can help you understand and plan your work.

By turning it on, you agree that:

  1. You will use AI help to learn, not to hand in work that isn't your own, and you will follow your school's and your teachers' rules about AI.
  2. You are responsible for how you use it. Canvas Harness and the people who make it are not responsible for any academic-honesty issue that comes from your use of AI help.
  3. What you send goes to the AI site you choose, under that site's own terms and privacy policy.
