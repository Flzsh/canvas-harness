Welcome to Canvas Harness

Canvas, within reach.

One question before you start: where is your school’s Canvas?

Copy it from your browser’s address bar when you are on Canvas. Most schools use an address ending in instructure.com. If yours has its own, such as canvas.your-school.edu, your browser will ask to allow Canvas Harness on that one site.

  • Canvas Harness runs only on your Canvas. It never changes submissions or grades.
  • Plans, notes and settings stay in this browser. There is no account to make.
  • Change the address any time from the Canvas Harness button in your browser’s toolbar.
